The
HyperLend Alerts Bot
is designed to keep you updated on critical activities within your HyperLend account. With customizable alerts for health factors, borrow rates, and liquidations, you can track specific addresses and receive timely notifications straight to your Telegram. Whether you're monitoring transactions or managing risk thresholds, this bot makes it easy to stay informed and respond quickly to changes that matter most.
We value your feedback, and any suggestions will be rewarded—just send us a DM on
Twitter
.
