# Points
The HyperLend points program began with 24.3.2025, points are designed to reward users who actively contribute to the success of the protocol.
Points are distributed on a **weekly basis**, with the distribution formula remaining undisclosed. The calculation methodology is updated on a recurring basis to align with protocol priorities and growth strategies.
**HyperLend reserves the right to modify past point distributions at its sole discretion.**
The official points season ended on Oct 22 2025.
