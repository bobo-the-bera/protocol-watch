You will need HPL in your Spot Balance on HyperEVM (if you hold HPL in HyperCore you can use our bridge to bridge it to HyperEVM).
Go to:
https://app.hyperlend.finance/staking
Choose the amount you'd like to stake.
Stake and approve in your wallet
Staking thas no lockup period.
To unstake, follow the same process in reverse
Navigate to the unstake
Choose the amount you'd like to ustake.
Unstake and approve in your wallet
For further information about borrowing discounts and staking you can refer to the
Staking
section.
