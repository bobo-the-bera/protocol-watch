To use the testnet faucet, you need to have deposited on mainnet with the same address. Users who have deposited on mainnet may then receive 1,000 mock USDC for testnet use:
https://app.hyperliquid-testnet.xyz/drip
If you are using an email login, Privy generates a different wallet address for mainnet and testnet. You can
Export your email wallet
from mainnet, import it into a wallet extension (e.g.,
Rabby
, MetaMask), and connect it to testnet.
