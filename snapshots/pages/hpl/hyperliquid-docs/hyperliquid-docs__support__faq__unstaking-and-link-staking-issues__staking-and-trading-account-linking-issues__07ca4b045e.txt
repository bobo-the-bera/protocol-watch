Do I need to link a trading and staking account if I trade and stake on the same account?
No, this linking feature is only for users who want to trade and stake on separate accounts
Can I change the trading account that is linked?
No, linking is permanent. Unlinking is not supported
Will my staking account continue to receive
staking discounts
?
No, the staking user will not receive any staking-related fee discount after being linked to a trading account
Why can’t I set up my staking and trading account link?
Linking requires the trading account to send an action first. The staking account then finalizes the link
Please refer to the Docs section for more information:
https://hyperliquid.gitbook.io/hyperliquid-docs/trading/fees#staking-linking
