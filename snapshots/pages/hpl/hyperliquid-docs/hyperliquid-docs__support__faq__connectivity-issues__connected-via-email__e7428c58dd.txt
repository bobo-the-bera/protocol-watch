What to do
If you flagged Privy messages as spam in the past, that would lead to your address being removed from their whitelist. You can reach out directly to Privy’s support team to ask them to check why your email isn’t receiving verification codes at
support@privy.io
and re-enable them.
In the future, once you’re able to login to your email wallet, you have the option to export your email wallet and import it into the wallet extension of your choice, so you no longer need to use email login:
https://hyperliquid.gitbook.io/hyperliquid-docs/onboarding/export-your-email-wallet
