If you accidentally send an unsupported asset to your defi wallet:
Make sure you are logged in with the same email address
Click "Export Email Wallet" in the settings dropdown in the navigation bar
Follow the steps in the pop-up to copy your private key
Import your private key into the wallet extension of your choice (e.g.,
https://support.rabby.io/en/articles/14120234-getting-started-with-rabby-extension#h_be51e8edb6
)
