Connected via an email account
Supports USDC deposits via Arbitrum, Base, Ethereum, and Polygon. If you sent a different asset, or sent USDC on any other network, it will not be credited. The USDC deposit flow is managed by Across Protocol, you can access your transfer history
here
Wrong deposit: If you deposited anything other than USDC on supported networks to the Across deposit address, your funds are automatically redirected to your email wallet within 10 minutes.
Deposited below 5 USDC: Your funds will not be credited, and are automatically redirected to your email wallet. Deposits below 1 USDC are lost.
For both cases, you can retrieve the funds from your email wallet by following the steps here:
https://hyperliquid.gitbook.io/hyperliquid-docs/onboarding/export-your-email-wallet
Legacy email deposit flow: If you sent USDC on Arbitrum to the old email deposit address that is generated by Privy, your deposit will still be processed. If your deposit does not show up, try refreshing the app.hyperliquid.xyz page. Your email wallet address is still your address on Hyperliquid, and you can export it here:
https://hyperliquid.gitbook.io/hyperliquid-docs/onboarding/export-your-email-wallet
