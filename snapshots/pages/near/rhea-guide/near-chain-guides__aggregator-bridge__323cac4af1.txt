How to bridge?
Step1: Click on the "Bridge" tab and choose Aggregate Bridge
Step2: Select the chain you want to bridge "from" and "to" for the asset you want to bridge.
Step3: Connect the wallet of the chain you selected.
Step4: Enter the amount of the assets you want to bridge.
The aggregator will automatically calculate and choose the best amount out.
Step 5: Click "
Preview"
to view the bridge preview.
Step 6: Click "
Transfer"
to sign transaction.
You can check the progress anytime through the "Bridge Transaction History" page
