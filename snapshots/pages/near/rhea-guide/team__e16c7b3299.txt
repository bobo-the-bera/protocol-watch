The team is a mix of both pseudonymous and non-pseudonymous people. Within the crypto space, being pseudonymous can mitigate the risks of conducting an activity that is not considered as regulated in a specific country, for example. The team does not stop to the above list. As a community-driven project, many community members are contributing, on a regular basis, to different aspects of the project, such as Marketing, end-to-end community testing, etc.
Moderators are also a key piece of the project. They participate in the success and growth of RHEA Finance in different areas, such as (not limited to):
User onboarding/acquisition
User support/guidance
Discussion facilitation
Removal of unrelated or inappropriate content
